Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Panel with all train types currently on rails, grouped by composition (shortcut button or Ctrl+Shift+Y).
    - Fuel selection from every fuel accepted by locomotives, with item icons.
    - Pick two rails or train stops on the map; the mod finds the shortest path with the game's own pathfinder.
    - Estimated travel time and fuel burned for the selected train types, using train physics (acceleration, braking, air resistance, fuel bonuses).
    - Selected train types and fuel are remembered between games.
